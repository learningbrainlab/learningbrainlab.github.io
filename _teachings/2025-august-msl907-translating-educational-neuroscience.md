#### Course Description

Efficacious translation from science of learning research to education practice and policy making continues to have challenges. Educational professionals need to have up-to-date knowledge of the ethics, feasibility, and challenges of translation to make informed decisions for their students.

#### Learning Outcomes

By the end of the course, participants are able to read and integrate science of learning research with a critical eye, compare different ways of turning research findings into classroom practice, and judge how well the evidence behind an educational intervention or claim actually supports its use with learners.
