Chiao-Yi Wu, Ph.D. (吳喬伊)

Adjunct Associate Professor
Center for General Education, National Cheng Kung University, Taiwan
Principal Investigator, The Learning Brain Lab

Email ORCID Google Scholar Web of Science LinkedIn

Chiao-Yi Wu is an Adjunct Associate Professor at the Center for General Education, National Cheng Kung University (NCKU), Taiwan, and the Principal Investigator of the Learning Brain Lab. Trained as a psychologist and cognitive neuroscientist, she studies how the brain supports learning, with a focus on language, reading, mathematics, and executive function in monolingual and bilingual learners.

Before joining NCKU in 2026, she was an Education Research Scientist at the Centre for Research in Child Development (CRCD), National Institute of Education, Nanyang Technological University (NTU), Singapore, and earlier a Research Scientist at the Centre for Research and Development in Learning (CRADLE@NTU). She completed her postdoctoral training in the Department of Neuropsychology at the Max Planck Institute for Human Cognitive and Brain Sciences in Leipzig, Germany. She holds a Ph.D. in Psychology from NTU Singapore and a B.Sc. in Psychology and Neurobiology & Cognitive Science from National Taiwan University.

Her research combines behavioural assessment with neuroimaging, including magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) and functional near-infrared spectroscopy (fNIRS), as well as neuroimaging meta-analysis. She has led and co-led research programmes funded by Singapore’s Ministry of Education, the National Research Foundation, the National Institute of Education, and Temasek Foundation.
