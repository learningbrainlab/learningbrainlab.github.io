**Meta-Analyses of the Neural Networks for Reading, Math, and Executive Function in Development (Meta-ReMaX)**

**Period:** 2022 – 2024  
**Funding:** Education Research Funding Programme, Ministry of Education, Singapore  
**Role:** Principal Investigator

#### Overview

Reading and mathematics are core foundations of learning, and both draw on executive function. This project uses neuroimaging meta-analysis to map the brain networks that support reading, math, and executive function, and to examine how these networks interact. We propose that executive function plays an important domain-general role in the development of both reading and math.

#### Why it matters

Understanding the shared and distinct brain networks behind reading and math can inform the identification of subtypes of learning difficulties and guide the design of interventions that strengthen literacy and numeracy in children, including approaches that target executive function.
