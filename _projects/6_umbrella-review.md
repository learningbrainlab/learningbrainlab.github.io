**Science of Learning-Based Interventions to Improve Literacy, Numeracy, and Well-being in Students: An Umbrella Review**

**Period:** 2024 – 2026  
**Funding:** Strategic Research Fund, Office of Education Research, National Institute of Education, Singapore  
**Role:** Co-PI (2024 – 2026)

#### Overview

Many meta-analyses have tested interventions grounded in the science of learning, but their findings are spread across different fields and outcomes. This umbrella review brings the evidence together, synthesising existing meta-analyses of interventions that aim to improve students' literacy, numeracy, and well-being.

#### Why it matters

A single, systematic overview helps educators and policymakers see which approaches are well supported, for whom, and where the evidence is still thin.
