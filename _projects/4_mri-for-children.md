**Magnetic Resonance Imaging for Children: A Guide for Parents**

#### What is MRI?

Magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) is a safe, non-invasive procedure that is widely used in hospitals and in research. It uses a strong magnetic field and radio waves, not X-rays, to take detailed pictures of the brain.

#### Helping children feel ready

Lying still inside a scanner can feel unfamiliar for young children. Our team developed the **MRI Space Mission**, a child-centred, step-by-step training protocol that prepares children for their brain scan through play and practice in a mock scanner, so that the real scan is a calmer and more comfortable experience.
