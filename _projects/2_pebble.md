**Promoting Effective Biliteracy and Balanced Bilingual Development in Early Childhood (PEBBLE)**

**Period:** 2019 – 2023  
**Funding:** Temasek Foundation, Singapore Millennium Foundation Grant  
**Role:** Principal Investigator

#### Overview

Many children in Singapore grow up learning to speak, read, and write in two languages. PEBBLE aimed to develop a systematic early screening and training programme that supports balanced bilingual development and biliteracy in early childhood.

#### Approach

The project took a multimodal approach, combining behavioural assessment with measures of brain and environment. Children were recruited in their final year of kindergarten (K2) and followed up in Primary 1. The project set out to:

- identify neural, cognitive, and environmental predictors of bilingual and biliteracy development in the preschool years, and
- evaluate how different training programmes influence children's biliteracy development.

#### Why it matters

Early identification of children who may struggle with one or both of their languages allows timely, targeted support before formal schooling begins.
