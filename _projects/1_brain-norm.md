**Understanding Brain Networks of Reading and Math to Inform Diagnostics and Interventions (Brain-NORM)**

**Period:** 2023 – 2027  
**Funding:** Ministry of Education, Singapore, Science of Learning Grant Call  
**Role:** Co-PI and Sub-Project PI (2023 – 2026); Collaborator (from 2026)

#### Overview

Brain-NORM investigates the brain networks that support reading and mathematics in children, and how these networks relate to working memory and other cognitive skills. The aim is to build an evidence base that can inform how learning difficulties are identified and supported.

#### Approach

The sub-project combines systematic review and neuroimaging meta-analysis with functional near-infrared spectroscopy (fNIRS) studies of young children, examining how reading and working memory networks develop and overlap.
